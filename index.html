About

I have worked in libraries since 2008, across public, academic, and archival settings. Since 2022 I have directed Nappanee Public Library in northern Indiana, which serves more than 10,000 residents in a rural community with a large Amish population.

My day job is mostly people, money, and public accountability. I lead a staff of more than 20, including 7 direct reports. I work closely with a Board of Trustees, two county governments, multiple township boards, and a school board, and I answer for how taxpayer money is spent. In the past few years that has meant initiating and winning approval for a $4.6M renovation and selecting, negotiating, and launching a new integrated library system. My training is in archives, history, and English.

What I like most is making something complicated easy for the person in front of me, and getting a room of people with different priorities moving in the same direction. I especially love the challenge of turning a skeptic into a champion.
